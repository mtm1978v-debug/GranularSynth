GranularSynth - Learning path (presets L01 to L37)
==================================================
Each preset demonstrates one principle. Load it, play it, and check in the "Modulation" tab
which matrix rows are in use. The red line under a control shows live how far the matrix is
currently shifting it (the effective value).
Formula for each row: effective value = base value + (Source x Factor + Offset) x Amount

Part 1 - Matrix basics
  L01  LFO -> Position. Hold a long note: the sound slowly wanders through the choir sample.
       Change the Amount in row 1 = how far it wanders.
  L02  LFO 1 creates vibrato, LFO 2 changes the rate of LFO 1. The vibrato breathes.
       An LFO can control any parameter - even another LFO.
  L03  Velocity -> Cutoff and Grain size. Play soft and loud: loud gets brighter and
       punchier. Negative Amount = opposite direction.
  L04  Note pitch -> Grain size and echo time. Low notes: large grains, long echo.
       Offset -50 % centres the source around the middle of the keyboard.
  L05  Mod wheel -> filter + macros "Movement" and "Space". Slowly raise the mod wheel:
       one control for the whole expression.
  L06  Aftertouch spreads out the choir, pitch bend bends only the echo.

Part 2 - Envelopes and triggers
  L07  Filter envelope -> Scatter: on the attack the grains explode and then contract.
       Envelopes can also be used for targets other than the filter.
  L08  Grain envelope -> Echo mix with negative Amount: while the note sounds it stays
       dry - on release the echo blooms.
  L09  LFO trigger "Note on": the saw restarts with every note -> every note gets the
       same filter sweep. Switch to "free" and compare.
  L10  LFO trigger "Note off": the release drops in pitch.
  L11  Arpeggiator: hold a chord - the notes play one after another over 2 octaves.
       In the step pattern (Extras tab) steps switched off = rests. Click the cells!
  L12  Factor x1/2 + Offset +50 % turn an LFO (-1..+1) into a unipolar source (0..1):
       reverse amount and width only swing upwards.

Part 3 - Sidechain
  Choosing the sidechain source: in GranularHost use "Sidechain" at the bottom (e.g. microphone),
  in FL Studio route a mixer channel to the plugin input. The monitor shows the sidechain
  signal in orange, IN the level, SC the envelope follower.
  L13  Ducking: signal on the sidechain makes the pad quieter - the classic pumping.
       Put a kick on the sidechain.
  L14  Auto-wah: the loudness of your voice opens the band-pass. Say "wah wah".
  L15  Echo pitch via mod wheel: down = normal echoes, up = each repeat up to one octave
       higher. Move it slowly while playing - the staircase gets steeper.
  L16  The level scrubs through the vowels a-e-i-o-u: quiet = a, loud = u.
  L17  Dub echo: feedback only while signal is coming in - echoes on call.
  L18  SC pitch -> Pitch via the matrix (Factor x2.65, Offset -125 %): the choir sings
       back the notes you sing into the microphone. Hold a key!
       Without a voice the choir sounds normal; with a voice it gets louder and follows it.
  L19  The same with "Auto-Pitch" in semitones, one octave higher, with vibrato.

Finale
  L20  Everything together: wandering position, LFO cascade, filter envelope, rising
       pitch echo, velocity, mod wheel (echo amount) and sidechain (pitch scatter).
       Hold long notes, move the mod wheel, speak into the microphone.

New features
  L21  Sidechain trigger (level): a loud shout into the microphone starts a One-shot LFO
       that plays a melody via the pitch matrix. Hold a key, then shout.
  L22  Sidechain trigger (tone): a sung note makes its major triad sound one note after
       another (Auto-Pitch in semitones + free LFO on Pitch).
  L23  Both conditions: only a loud AND high note opens the filter once.
       Sing quiet and high or loud and low: nothing happens.
  L24  Glide (portamento): every note glides 0.28 s from the previous one, the
       sine oscillator glides along. Change the glide time in the Synth tab.
  L25  Oscillator FM with its own FM envelope: the attack sounds metallic, the timbre
       quickly softens while the note keeps sounding - a bell.
  L26  Filter per note: each note has its own filter with its own filter envelope.
       Strike the chord notes one after another (1 s apart) and hold them: each note
       slowly opens on its own, the ones already sounding stay where they are.

Part 4 - Start, Stop and time ("LFO 1-4 / Trigger" and "Timing / Events" tabs)
  In the name: "FM" = oscillators sound too, "Grains" = grains sound too.
  An LFO with a start event runs until its Stop halts it (then no modulation).
  L27  Stop "Note off": vibrato per note, on release the tail is calm.
       Set Stop to "never": the vibrato keeps running in the tail.
  L28  Stop "after time" 1/2: the wobble on the FM amount runs for only a half note.
       Change the tempo - the length follows the beat.
  L29  Delay + Stop: the tremolo kicks in after 1/4 (Timing / Events) and stops
       after 1/1 - the run time counts from the delayed start.
  L30  Stop after 120 ms: a fast pitch drop on the attack only. Change the ms value.
  L31  Three oscillators, each with its own envelope (Osc 1-3 tabs): the sine swells
       slowly, FM plucks briefly, filtered noise breathes.
  L32  Osc 2 envelope as matrix source: Osc 2 is muted, its short envelope makes the
       FM attack of Osc 1 bright. Change the decay of Osc 2.
  L33  Start "Note off" + Stop after 1/1: on release the grains sparkle for exactly
       one bar, then it fades out calmly.
  L34  LFO chain via the Partner selection (field below "One-shot"): hold a note for 3 bars.
       Bar 1: LFO 1 opens the filter. Bar 2: LFO 2 (Partner LFO 1) makes the FM amount
       swell and fade. Bar 3: LFO 3 (Partner LFO 2) sweeps across the stereo panorama.
       All three are set to "One-shot" - only the end of a One-shot triggers the Partner.
       LFO 2 and 3 have Stop "Note off": that way they wait until their start.

Part 5: free macros (Modulation tab, to the right of the matrix)
  L35  Macro 1 with S-curve: hold a note and slowly raise Macro 1 - filter, grains,
       width and reverb open up together. The targets are listed below the macro.
  L36  Macro 2, Curve "Slow rise": at first little happens, at the top the choir falls apart
       (scatter, reverse, pitch randomness, echo). Density has a negative Amount.
  L37  Two macros for the DAW: Macro 1 = wobble faster and brighter, Macro 3 = echo.
       In FL: right-click the macro control > Automate in DAW.
